What kind of projects do you take on?

From websites and brand identity to analytics dashboards, AI integrations, automation, and digital growth strategy. If your challenge crosses disciplines, we can discuss a tailored scope.

Can we work together internationally?

Yes. DataWebX works with clients remotely and can organize communication and milestones around different time zones.

How does a project begin?

Start with a conversation about your goals. We'll clarify requirements, propose an approach, and agree on scope and timelines before work begins.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Ongoing support and retainer arrangements can be discussed based on the needs of your project.

How many revisions are included?

Revision rounds depend on the package and agreed project scope.

What is the typical turnaround time?

Small projects typically take 3–5 business days; larger projects can take 1–3 weeks, depending on scope. Package-specific delivery times are listed above.

Do you offer monthly retainers?

Yes. Ongoing content creation, social media management, dashboards, and AI maintenance can be arranged on a monthly retainer.

How can I pay?

Payment options and milestones are confirmed before your project begins.
